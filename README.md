# Ezfi's Relics
A STS2 mods that adds 14 new relics to the shared relic pool.
All relics can be enabled or disabled in the Mod Configuration settings. It won't remove it from the compendium, but it will keep them out of runs.

## Common Relics
- Bloody Blade: When an enemy goes under half HP, inflict 2 Vulnerable.
- Heavy Shield: At the start of your turn, gain Block equal to half the highest enemy Block.
- Leaking Battery: At the end of your turn, deal 3 damage to a random enemy for each unspent Energy.
- Long Tooth: +1 Strength every 2 Turns.
- Protein Powder: Before your turn, halve your Frail and Weak.
- Scalpel: At the start of your turn, exhaust an unplayable card in your hand. If you do, draw 1 card.
- Snecko Dice: Enchant an Attack with Six-Sided (Gains 1-6 extra damage, the amount randomizes each time it's drawn)

## Uncommon Relics
- Grandfather Clock: On your 12th turn, the first 12 cards you play cost 0.
- Glock: If an attack does 50+ damage, double it.
- Hole-In-One Club: If you achieve victory on your 1st turn, gain 8 Max HP.
- Vampire Dentures: When you overkill an enemy by 15+ HP, heal 5 HP.
  
## Rare Relics
- Fax Machine: Card rewards will have an extra option from other colors.
- Overflowing Cup: Overkill damage is dealt to a random enemy.

## Shop-Only Relics
- Lovely Locket: Enchant a Power with Bonded (Gains Innate, cost increases by 1)
